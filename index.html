A few practical answers before you put an enquiry together.

Start a conversation
Who is this learning support for?

The sample programme covers broad primary and early secondary topics. A real provider should replace these examples with the levels and subjects they actually teach.

Can I book or pay for lessons here?

No. This is a website template demonstration. You can explore sample subjects and prepare an unsent enquiry; there is no live calendar, lesson booking or payment service.

What information should I include?

An adult contact name, reply email, broad subject and general goals are enough for this demonstration. Please leave out children’s names, dates of birth, school records and other personal details.

Are lessons online or in person?

Both options appear as preferences in this sample. Actual locations, lesson availability and teaching arrangements need confirmation by a real provider.
